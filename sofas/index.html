Find the Right Sofa for Your Living Room

A sofa is an important part of a comfortable living room. Explore different sofa styles and practical ideas to help you choose seating that suits your space, comfort needs and home design.

Choose a Sofa That Fits Your Space

Before choosing a sofa, measure your living room and consider the available floor space. A well-sized sofa can make a room feel welcoming without making it crowded.

Consider Comfort and Everyday Use

Think about how your sofa will be used every day. Seat depth, cushioning, back support and durable upholstery are useful factors to consider when selecting living room furniture.

Explore Different Sofa Styles

From simple designs to larger family seating options, choose a sofa style that complements your room layout. Neutral colours can create a timeless look, while accent colours can add personality.

Pair Your Sofa with the Right Coffee Table

A coffee table can complement your sofa and make your living room more functional. Consider the sofa's size, seating arrangement and available floor space when choosing a matching table. Explore our Coffee Tables guide for ideas about different styles, materials and storage options.
